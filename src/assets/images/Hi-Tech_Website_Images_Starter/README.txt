NOT INCLUDED, AND WHY
  Machine photographs supplied by the machine makers, as they are the vendors' own images.
  The solar panel and electric truck pictures, as they look like stock images.
  Slide pictures with headings printed on them, as the text cannot be removed cleanly.
  Customer logos, certificates and testimonial letters. Proper logo files will follow after the permission check.
  Charts, maps and sales figures, which are not going on the website.

WHAT IS STILL NEEDED
  Hero shots at 1920 x 1080 or larger, with clear space on one side for the headline.
  Clean single product shots for radiators, tanks and galvanising, with nothing stacked in storage and nothing lying around.
  Leadership photographs on a plain background, all with the same crop.
  Client logos as vector files or PNG with a transparent background.

BEFORE ANY OF THESE GO LIVE
  Photographs showing identifiable people need their consent.
  Anything marked REVIEW needs a decision from our side first.
